\load[turtle]

Turtle graphics can be embedded directly in normal OpTeX text.\par
The drawing starts at the current \TeX\ position and uses the current
pen state.\par

\def\square#1 {\repeat 4 {\forward #1 \right 90 }}
\def\flower#1 #2 {\repeat 36 {\right #1 \square #2 }}

\begturtle
\square 45
\endturtle
The document continues normally after the drawing.
\begturtle
\flower 15 20
\endturtle

\bigskip
\begturtle
\text{\llap A} \right 90 \forward 100
\text B \right 90 \forward 100
\text C \right 90 \back 10
\endturtle

\bye
